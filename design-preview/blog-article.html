UK Skilled Worker Visa Salary Threshold 2026 — Full List by Job

Complete 2026 breakdown: the £41,700 general minimum, going rates by occupation code, the new-entrant discount and how salary is actually calculated.

Key takeaways
  • You must clear three salary tests at once — general minimum, going rate, and £15.88/hour.
  • The 2026 general minimum is £41,700; new entrants and ISL roles drop to £33,400.
  • Only basic gross salary counts — bonuses, overtime and allowances don’t.

The Skilled Worker visa is the UK’s main work route, and in 2026 the salary rules are stricter than ever. If your salary doesn’t clear the threshold, your application is refused — no judgement, no appeal on that ground. This guide walks you through every threshold that applies in April 2026, who each one hits, and how to check whether your specific job code qualifies.

£41,700The 2026 general minimum salary for new Skilled Worker applicants.
If your salary falls below all three thresholds, your application is refused at the salary stage — with no judgement and no appeal on that ground.

The three numbers you need to know

There is no single “Skilled Worker salary threshold.” Your application must clear three separate salary tests simultaneously:

  1. General minimum salary — £41,700 per year for most new applicants from April 2026.
  2. Going rate for your occupation code — varies by SOC 2020 job code, published by the Home Office.
  3. £15.88 per hour — minimum hourly rate regardless of contract type.

You must meet all three. The Home Office takes the highest as your effective threshold — a nurse on £32,000 fails even if nursing’s going rate is lower, because the general minimum isn’t met (unless they qualify for a discount).

Who qualifies for a lower threshold?

Five groups get a discount from the £41,700 headline figure:

New entrants (under 26, recent graduate, or from a Student visa)£33,400Max 4 years on this rate

The new-entrant discount is the most commonly missed. If you’re 25, graduated within the last 2 years, or switching from a UK Student visa — apply it. It saves £8,300 per year on the threshold.

New-entrant beats ISL on going-rate share. Both share the £33,400 floor, but new-entrant needs only 70% of your occupation’s going rate, while ISL still requires 100%.

How salary is calculated — the gotchas

The Home Office doesn’t take your offer letter at face value. Only basic gross salary counts — bonuses, commission, overtime, most allowances, in-kind benefits and pension contributions do not. Salary is assessed against a standard 37.5-hour week, so part-time roles are pro-rated up, which is why the £15.88/hour floor exists.

Your salary-check workflow

  1. Find your SOC 2020 code. Your sponsor should know it; the ONS SOC 2020 tool is the definitive source.
  2. Check your going rate. The Home Office Appendix Skilled Occupations lists every code and its published rate.
  3. Confirm your threshold group. New entrant? ISL? STEM PhD? Apply the most generous discount.
  4. Verify £15.88/hour against your actual contracted weekly hours, not the full-time equivalent.

Document checklist before you apply

  • Current passport valid for the visa duration
  • Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) from your employer
  • Signed offer letter showing gross annual salary
  • English language evidence (B2 or higher)

Common questions

Can bonuses top up my offer to clear £41,700?

No. Only guaranteed basic gross salary counts. A £36,000 base plus bonuses still fails the test — the bonus is ignored.

What if my salary rises after I’m granted the visa?

Your threshold is locked at the date your CoS is issued. You’re fine for your current leave, but an extension or switch is assessed at the rate in force then.

Does London weighting count toward the threshold?

In most cases, no — London weighting and similar allowances are excluded unless guaranteed and stated in the Certificate of Sponsorship.
