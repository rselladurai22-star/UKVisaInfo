Why we exist

Most people don't have a tax adviser or an immigration solicitor on call. They have a question — “how much will I actually take home?”, “do I qualify for this visa?” — and a government page written for civil servants, not for them.

We read the primary source so you don't have to, and give you the answer first: the number, the rule, the deadline — then the detail underneath if you want it.

How we source everything

Every figure on UKDesk traces back to an official record. We don't estimate, and we don't repeat what other blogs say. Where a rule has an exception, we link to the exact page that says so.

  • Rates and thresholds from GOV.UK, HMRC and the Bank of England
  • Each page carries a visible “last updated” date and its sources
  • Calculations are reviewed against the official worked examples

How we stay free

UKDesk is funded by a few unobtrusive ads on some pages — never inside a calculation, never a pop-up. No accounts, no email harvesting, no consultancy funnel. If we ever can't keep it clean, we'll change how it's funded, not the experience.
