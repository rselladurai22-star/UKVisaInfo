Skilled Worker visa

Last updated 14 May 2026 · Source: GOV.UK — Skilled Worker visa
The short answer

The Skilled Worker visa lets you work for a UK employer who holds a sponsor licence. You'll usually need a job offer at or above £41,700 a year (or the going rate for the role, whichever is higher), English at B1, and enough savings to support yourself. It leads to settlement after 5 years.

£769+Visa fee (3 yrs)

~3 wksDecision time

Who it's for

It's for people with a confirmed job offer from a Home Office–licensed sponsor, in an eligible occupation. Most roles at RQF level 3 (A-level equivalent) and above qualify, from nursing to software engineering.

How to apply

  • Get a Certificate of SponsorshipYour employer issues a CoS with the job and salary details.
  • Check the pointsYou need 70 points — sponsorship, salary, English and the right job all count.
  • Apply online & payVisa fee + the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) up front.
  • Prove your identityVia the UKVI app or at a visa centre, with biometrics.
  • Wait for the decisionAbout 3 weeks from outside the UK, 8 weeks from inside.

Common questions

Can I bring my family?

Yes — your partner and children under 18 can usually join you as dependants. Each pays their own visa fee and IHS.

Does the £41,700 apply to every job?

No. It's the general threshold from April 2025, but some roles use a different “going rate”, and new entrants or shortage roles can qualify on less. Use the points checker to be sure.

Does time on this visa count towards settlement?

Yes. After 5 continuous years you can usually apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR).
